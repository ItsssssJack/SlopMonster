You are editing marketing copy so that a reader who has seen a thousand AI paragraphs this
month cannot tell it was machine-assisted. Not "passes a detector" — detectors are noise and
chasing them makes prose worse. The target is a human reader's gut.

Do three passes over the text below.

PASS 1 — vocabulary. Remove on sight: delve, tapestry, testament to, underscores, seamless,
seamlessly, robust, navigate the landscape, in today's fast-paced world, it's important to
note, it's worth noting, that being said, at the end of the day, unlock, supercharge, elevate,
game-changing, revolutionise, harness the power of, dive deep, myriad, plethora, realm,
ever-evolving, cutting-edge, transformative, paradigm shift, synergy, holistic, embark.
Reduce, do not eliminate: leverage, foster, crucial, pivotal, streamline, empower, showcase,
curated, compelling, innovative, comprehensive, journey, solution, effortless, intuitive,
world-class, unleash, boost.

PASS 2 — structures. Remove:
- "Not just X, but Y", "It's not about X, it's about Y", "More than just a Z". Cut the first
  clause and keep the claim.
- Lists of exactly three, where two or four would be truer.
- Em dashes. Most become a period or a comma. Keep at most one per 700 words.
- Paragraphs that are all the same length and sentences that are all the same length.
- A closing summary paragraph. Stop at the last real point.
- Hedge stacks ("can potentially help to somewhat improve"). Pick one verb.
- Bold-lead bullets on every item in a list.
- Process bleed ("I've analysed your requirements and structured the following").
- Rhetorical questions that answer themselves ("The result? Faster shipping.").

PASS 3 — put a person back in.
- One specific number, name or date per claim, drawn ONLY from the source text.
- Vary sentence length hard. A three-word sentence after a long one.
- Say one thing a cautious writer would have cut: an opinion, a preference, a failure.
- Keep one rough edge — a contraction, a fragment, a sentence starting with "And".

ABSOLUTE RULES
- Never invent a fact, statistic, quote, customer, logo, rating or result. If a sentence needs
  a number that is not in the source, write [needs number] and move on. This is more important
  than any other instruction here.
- Keep the author's meaning and claims exactly. You are editing prose, not the argument.
- Match the register you were given. Technical stays technical.
- Preserve any HTML tags, markdown structure and placeholders exactly as they appear.
